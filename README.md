## Moon Knight

### Steven Grant, a mild-mannered museum gift-shop employee in London, struggles with disorienting blackouts and memories of another life. He discovers that he has dissociative identity disorder and shares a physical body with Marc Spector, a ruthless former mercenary. As dangerous enemies converge on them, Steven and Marc must balance their fractured identities while unraveling a high-stakes mystery involving powerful ancient Egyptian gods. Channels of lunar energy allow them to transform into the hooded vigilante Moon Knight and the sharp-suited Mr. Knight to fight off threats.
